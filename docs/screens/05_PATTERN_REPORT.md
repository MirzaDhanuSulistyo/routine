# SCR-05: Weekly Pattern & Anomaly Report

**Route/deep link:** `routine://insights`  
**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web  
**PRD requirements:** FR-6  

## Purpose

Displays weekly operational insights generated by the local rule-based anomaly engine. Highlights routine shifts, missed responsibilities, repeated free-text observations, and candidate correlation trends (e.g. "Late clock-outs coincided with missed math practice on 3 of last 4 Tuesdays").

## Entry points

- Bottom Tab Bar ("Insights / Report")
- Weekly summary notification

## Content hierarchy

1. **Header**: Weekly Period Selector (e.g. "Jul 14 – Jul 20, 2026").
2. **Weekly Summary Stats**: Total planned items, completion rate %, missed count.
3. **Detected Anomalies & Timing Shifts Card**:
   - Items completed >15 minutes late.
   - Repeated notes/observations (e.g. "Car noise noted 2 times").
4. **Candidate Correlation Card**:
   - Highlighted relationships formatted cautiously: *"Observed relationship: Late work clock-out coincided with missed math practice."*
5. **Actionable Suggestions**: Option to adjust routine reminder times based on actual completion patterns.
